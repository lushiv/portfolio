Personal story / Darchula, Nepal

From a mountain village to building systems.

A first look at the internet became a question that shaped my path: how does this work?

I was born on 18 May 1998 in Darchula, a far-off hill region of Nepal. There was no easy access to modern technology or internet, and the idea of building systems for the world felt like a story from another life.

My turning point came during Dashain holidays when my cousin, studying in Mahendranagar, showed me Facebook on a Nokia C201 using UC Browser. I still remember that moment: the first opening of a social network, the first internet window, and the beginning of a deeper question: how does this work?

With his help, I created my first Facebook account. It was a small act, but it started a long chain of learning. I wanted to understand the internet, the code behind websites, the logic behind digital systems, and the ways technology could connect people across distance.

That curiosity led me into computer repair, mobile sales, laptop servicing, and later internet research. I learned by doing, by testing, by fixing small problems, and by staying patient through ambiguity. My story is not a dramatic shortcut; it is a practical climb. Step by step, I built the path from a village context to a global technology career.

The path so far

From repair work to systems engineering

Each stage added a different way of understanding technology, from devices and users to services and distributed systems.

2011

The first spark

Seeing Facebook on a basic phone was the moment the digital world became tangible. It changed how I saw possibility.

2014 to 2017

Hands-on learning

I learned through hardware and laptop service work, understanding how devices worked before understanding systems at scale.

2018 to 2020

Research before engineering

I moved into internet research, investigating AI, blockchain, and emerging technical landscapes. That deepened my ability to read technology trends and reason about systems.

2020 to present

Engineering and architecture

I build production backends, blockchain services, AI integrations, and distributed systems with attention to reliability and real-world use.

The question that stayed

How does this work?

It started with a glimpse of the internet in Darchula. Today, the same curiosity drives me to understand the systems behind products and build technology that works beyond the ideal conditions of a lab.

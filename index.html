01 / About

From a remote village to global systems thinking

I'm Janak Singh Raikhola, Founder & CEO at FutureStoreAI and a backend systems architect based in Nepal. I build the services behind products: APIs, distributed systems, event-driven workflows, data platforms and the infrastructure that keeps them reliable.

My backend work spans REST and gRPC APIs, microservices, PostgreSQL, MySQL, MongoDB and Redis, with asynchronous messaging through Kafka and RabbitMQ. I also build Web3 integrations with Ethereum, Solana, Web3.js, dApps and Solidity smart contracts, alongside blockchain payment and wallet workflows.

I work across cloud and DevOps with AWS, Google Cloud, DigitalOcean, Docker, Kubernetes, CI/CD and observability tools, and apply secure API practices including OAuth, JWT and TLS. My research also explores AI agents, IoT and robotics: connecting software to devices such as Arduino and ESP32, using MQTT and edge computing, and experimenting with ROS 2, sensors and automation.
